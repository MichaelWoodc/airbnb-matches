Sources and interpretation

Only explicitly user-confirmed photo matches are classified. License not shown means no number found in the saved Airbnb listing text, not a fresh live-page inspection. A missing license record is a screening flag. Current certificate status can be checked with the licensing agency.

Jurisdiction is determined from the matched property coordinates and official SAGIS municipality polygons, not its postal city. Unit conflicts, missing location evidence, conflicting property matches, and unresolved license coverage can require review. License status and expiration are preserved from the source records.
